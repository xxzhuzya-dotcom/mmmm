Quiet design, built to last.

Maison Aurel began as a small workshop with a simple conviction: a piece of furniture should become better with age. Every design starts with proportion and restraint, and ends with a finish you can feel.

We work with a small circle of master joiners and upholsterers. Each piece is made to order, signed by the craftsperson who built it, and delivered with a lifetime care promise.

Honest materials

Responsibly sourced oak, walnut and marble, chosen for grain and character, never veneered over.

Hand-finished

Traditional joinery, oiled surfaces and hand-stitched upholstery, with no shortcuts.

Made to order

Choose your timber, fabric and dimensions. Most pieces are ready in ten to fourteen weeks.
